Bullet-Fuselage Interceptor Quadcopter

SolidWorks 3D Printing Aerodynamics
[ Image — CAD render or build photo goes here ]
Objective

Design a cylindrical, bullet-shaped quadcopter — modeled after Ukrainian interceptor drones — optimized for efficient forward cruise rather than pure hover, with an eye toward eventual humanitarian payload delivery.

Constraints
  • Modular three-section body for manufacturability and repair-in-the-field access
  • Tractor motor configuration for cruise efficiency over hover-optimized layouts
  • 5-inch props, 1900kv motors —
Key decisions

Center of gravity placed at or below the thrust plane for passive pendulum stability. Arms use a shear-taper airfoiled planform at roughly 20° sweep in the current design, weighed against a simpler straight rectangular carbon-fiber-filled arm — the latter wins for a faster, high-speed proof-of-concept build despite the aerodynamic trade-off. Tail fins rotated 45° relative to the arms for prop clearance. Payload is released via a multi-bay sequential mechanism rather than a single drop, for controlled, staged delivery.

3D-Printed Flying Wing (B-2 Style)

ArduPilot RC Aircraft 3D Printing
[ Image — build or flight photo goes here ]
Objective

Build a ~1m-wingspan, single pusher-motor flying wing as a first full aircraft project and a deliberate stepping stone toward an eventual tail-sitter VTOL design.

Constraints
  • Fully 3D-printed airframe
  • Off-the-shelf flight stack: ArduPilot on a Matek F405-Wing controller
  • RadioMaster transmitter with ExpressLRS, 4S LiPo power
Key decisions

Chose a tailless flying-wing layout specifically to build fluency in ArduPilot tuning and reflex/elevon control before attempting the added complexity of a tail-sitter VTOL transition.
